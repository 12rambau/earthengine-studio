# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We want participation in Earth Engine Studio to be welcoming, constructive, and safe for everyone. Contributors, maintainers, and community members are expected to treat each other with respect, regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

- Be considerate in issues, pull requests, reviews, and other project spaces.
- Discuss ideas and technical decisions in good faith.
- Give and receive feedback constructively.
- Respect different experiences, perspectives, and levels of familiarity with the project.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or imagery in project spaces.
- Trolling, insults, or sustained disruption of discussions.
- Sharing another person's private information without permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional community.

## Reporting and Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately using the contact details on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/12rambau). Do not open a public issue for a conduct report.

Maintainers will review reports promptly and handle them as confidentially as practical. Depending on the circumstances, actions may include a private warning, removal of content, temporary restrictions, or a permanent ban from project spaces.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces and when someone is representing Earth Engine Studio in a public context.
